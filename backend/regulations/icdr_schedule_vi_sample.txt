[CLAUSE 1(a)]
The offer document shall state the full legal name of the issuer company exactly as it
appears on the certificate of incorporation, together with its Corporate Identity Number.

[CLAUSE 1(b)]
The offer document shall disclose the Corporate Identity Number (CIN) of the issuer as
allotted by the Registrar of Companies, and shall confirm it matches the incorporation
certificate on record.

[CLAUSE 1(c)]
The offer document shall state the registered office address of the issuer, and shall note
any change of registered office in the preceding three years along with reasons therefor.

[CLAUSE 1(d)]
The offer document shall state the date and place of incorporation of the issuer, and the
date on which it was converted, if applicable, from one class of company to another.

[CLAUSE 2(a)]
The offer document shall disclose the full name of each promoter, exactly as it appears on
their Permanent Account Number, and their relationship (if any) to other promoters.

[CLAUSE 2(b)]
The offer document shall disclose the Permanent Account Number (PAN) of each promoter.

[CLAUSE 2(c)]
Where a promoter also holds a directorship, the offer document shall disclose the
Director Identification Number (DIN) of such promoter-director.

[CLAUSE 6(iii)]
The offer document shall describe the nature of the issuer's business in plain terms,
including the principal business activity and the class of customers served.

[CLAUSE 6(iv)]
The offer document shall list the issuer's key products or services, and the proportion of
revenue each contributes where such information is available.

[CLAUSE 6(v)]
The offer document shall disclose the location of each manufacturing unit or principal
place of operation of the issuer.

[CLAUSE 6(vii)]
The offer document shall disclose the total number of employees of the issuer as on the
date of the offer document.

[CLAUSE 9(i)]
The offer document shall state the total proposed issue size, expressed in rupees crore,
distinguishing between fresh issue and offer for sale components, if any.

[CLAUSE 9(ii)]
The offer document shall set out the objects of the issue, describing each specific purpose
for which the net proceeds are intended to be utilised.

[CLAUSE 9(iii)]
The offer document shall provide a schedule of utilisation of issue proceeds, indicating the
year-wise or milestone-wise deployment of funds for each stated object.

[CLAUSE 11(ix)]
The offer document shall state the percentage shareholding of the promoter(s) in the
issuer, both pre-issue and post-issue.

[CLAUSE 11(x)]
The offer document shall disclose the history of the promoter's acquisition of shares in the
issuer, including the mode of acquisition (subscription at incorporation, transfer, rights
issue, bonus issue, or otherwise) and the relevant dates.

[CLAUSE 11(xi)]
The offer document shall state the number of equity shares held by the promoter that are
eligible for and subject to the minimum promoter contribution lock-in requirement.

[CLAUSE 14(i)]
The offer document shall disclose whether the issuer has any related party relationships,
including subsidiaries, associate companies, or promoter group entities.

[CLAUSE 14(ii)]
The offer document shall describe all related party transactions entered into by the issuer
during the three financial years preceding the offer document, including the nature,
amount, and parties involved in each transaction.

[CLAUSE 17(i)]
The offer document shall disclose all material operational risks specific to the issuer,
including risks relating to supply chain concentration, dependence on key customers, or
dependence on key managerial personnel, quantified where the issuer is able to do so.

[CLAUSE 17(ii)]
The offer document shall disclose material industry-wide or regulatory risks that could
affect the issuer's business, avoiding generic industry commentary in favour of risks
specific to the issuer's sector and operations.

[CLAUSE 17(iii)]
The offer document shall disclose all material litigation, regulatory action, or disciplinary
proceedings involving the issuer, its promoters, or its directors, or shall affirmatively state
that no such proceedings exist.

[CLAUSE REG229(4)]
For SME issuers under Chapter IX of these Regulations, the offer document shall disclose
material outstanding litigation involving the issuer, its promoters, its directors, or its group
companies, and shall not omit litigation on the ground of immateriality without stating the
basis for that assessment.

[CLAUSE CAPITAL-STRUCTURE-1]
The offer document shall state the authorised share capital of the issuer as at the date of
the offer document, and the number and face value of shares comprising it.

[CLAUSE CAPITAL-STRUCTURE-2]
The offer document shall state the issued, subscribed, and paid-up share capital of the
issuer, and shall reconcile any difference between authorised and paid-up capital.

[CLAUSE CAPITAL-STRUCTURE-3]
The offer document shall present the build-up of the issuer's share capital in tabular form,
showing the date of allotment, number of shares allotted, face value, issue price, and
nature of consideration for each allotment since incorporation.

[CLAUSE 4(i)]
The offer document shall disclose the composition of the board of directors, stating for
each director their name, designation, and whether they are a promoter, executive, or
independent director.

[CLAUSE 4(ii)]
The offer document shall disclose the issuer's key managerial personnel, including the
chief financial officer and company secretary, together with their designation and
relevant experience.

[CLAUSE 4(iii)]
The offer document shall disclose any relationship, by blood or marriage, between any two
directors or between a director and a promoter of the issuer, or shall affirmatively state
that no such relationship exists.

[CLAUSE 4(iv)]
The offer document shall disclose any change in the composition of the board of directors
or key managerial personnel during the three years preceding the offer document, stating
the nature of and reason for each change.

[CLAUSE 15(i)]
The offer document shall list all material licenses, registrations, and statutory approvals
held by the issuer that are necessary to carry on its business, together with their validity
period where applicable.

[CLAUSE 15(ii)]
The offer document shall disclose any material license, registration, or approval applied
for by the issuer that remains pending as at the date of the offer document, or shall
affirmatively state that none is pending.

[CLAUSE 16(i)]
The offer document shall disclose the issuer's outstanding borrowings as at the date of the
offer document, including the name of each lender, the amount outstanding, and the
security (if any) offered against each borrowing.

[CLAUSE 16(ii)]
The offer document shall disclose all material contingent liabilities of the issuer,
including guarantees given on behalf of third parties and disputed tax demands or claims
not acknowledged as debt, or shall affirmatively state that none exist.
