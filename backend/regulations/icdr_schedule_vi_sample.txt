Source: SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018,
Schedule VI Part A — "Disclosures in offer document/letter of offer"
[See Regulations 17, 24(2)(b), 57(1)(f), 70(2), 122(2)(ii), 175(1)(f), 239,
246(2)(b), 282(1)(f), 287(2), 291], cross-checked against a merchant banker's
regulation-wise compliance checklist for an SME IPO. Clause text below is
paraphrased, not quoted verbatim, and grouped by the real Part A item
numbering — this is a working subset covering the disclosure areas this
project currently drafts, not the full Schedule VI.

[CLAUSE 1(a)]
Front cover pages of the offer document shall state the name of the issuer,
its logo, the date and place of its incorporation, its corporate identity
number, and the address of its registered and corporate offices.

[CLAUSE 4(A)]
The Offer Document Summary shall state, in not more than 100 words, the
primary business of the issuer and the industry in which it operates.

[CLAUSE 4(B)]
The Offer Document Summary shall state the names of the promoter(s) of the
issuer.

[CLAUSE 4(F)]
The Offer Document Summary shall include, from the restated financial
statements for the past three years and stub period, a tabular summary
covering share capital, net worth, revenue, profit after tax, earnings per
share, net asset value per equity share, and total borrowings as per the
balance sheet.

[CLAUSE 4(J)]
The Offer Document Summary shall include a summary table of contingent
liabilities, cross-referenced to the contingent liabilities disclosed in the
issuer's restated financial statements.

[CLAUSE 4(K)]
The Offer Document Summary shall include a summary of related party
transactions for the preceding three years, cross-referenced to the related
party transactions disclosed in the issuer's restated financial statements.

[CLAUSE 5(B)]
Risk factors shall be classified into those which are specific to the
project and internal to the issuer, and those which are external to the
issuer and beyond its control.

[CLAUSE 5(G)]
Risk factors shall necessarily disclose, wherever applicable: material
statutory clearances and approvals yet to be received by the issuer; a
summary of all outstanding litigation and other material matters, in
tabular form with amounts involved where quantifiable, separately
highlighting any criminal, regulatory, or taxation matters that could have
a material adverse effect on the issuer; and dependence of the issuer on a
single customer or a small number of customers where loss of that
relationship could have a material adverse effect.

[CLAUSE 7(A)]
The General Information section shall state the name and address of the
issuer's registered and corporate offices, its registration number, and the
address of the Registrar of Companies where the issuer is registered.

[CLAUSE 7(B)]
The General Information section shall state the name, designation, address,
and Director Identification Number (DIN) of each member of the issuer's
board of directors.

[CLAUSE 7(C)]
The General Information section shall state the names, addresses, telephone
numbers, and e-mail addresses of the Company Secretary, legal advisor, and
bankers to the issuer.

[CLAUSE 8(A)]
The Capital Structure section shall state, in tabular form, the authorised,
issued, subscribed, and paid-up capital of the issuer, and the size of the
present issue including any promoters' contribution and net offer amount.

[CLAUSE 8(B)]
The Capital Structure section shall include the total shareholding of each
promoter in tabular form — nature of issue, date of allotment or transfer,
number of shares, face value, issue price, and percentage of pre- and
post-issue capital — together with details of promoters' contribution and
the lock-in period applicable to each promoter's holding.

[CLAUSE 9(A)]
The Particulars of the Issue section shall set out the objects of the issue
and, where relevant, a schedule for utilisation of the issue proceeds
including the basis of estimation and reasons for the funding requirement.
